# Changelog

## v0.4.0 - 2026-11-28

Semver level: MINOR (v0.4.0). Sixteen sizes that carried komando's page words leave `G`. The removal is breaking at MINOR level: nothing in Glamor reads them, and Glamor is used only inside Efolusi for now, as in v0.3.0. The look changes too: buttons become capsules (GL41), and two new marks arrive (GL36). The Mac sidebar arrives as parts (GL31 to GL34, GL37, GL38), and `SidebarItem` also exists for iPad and iPhone (GL39).

What is proven and what is not. CI proves the Mac build and tests and the generic iOS build, and since GL56 it also builds the iOS tests (`build-for-testing`) without running them. The window-check lines for the new scenes (`sidebar-a`, `sidebar-b`, `profile`, `marks`) and for the `components` additions (the ToggleCard switch, `button-shape`, the Deploy label inset) ran once in a real window, in the owner's strict run of 2026-11-28 (469 lines passed, 14 failed, 7 were unproven). The failures were lines about the switch, the marks, the folded header's value and the Tab order; the check looked them up by a role or a state the window did not have (see the entry on the window check below), and none is yet shown to be a drawn defect. They are proven only by the owner's next run of `scripts/check-window.sh --strict`, and the v0.4.0 tag waits for that run. The iOS sidebar row is compiled and source-pinned only; it has never run on a device or in a simulator.

- **Breaking: 16 komando page names are out of `G` (GL42).** Removed from `G.Width`: `hostName`, `connName`, `secretName`, `jobLog`, `cacheKeys`, `forwardName`, `forwardKind`, `updateKind`. Removed from `G.Resize`: `recipes`, `jobs`, `audit`, `filesPane`, `filesTransfers`, `querySplit`, `cacheSplit`, `terminalSplit`. `G.Resize.areas` is now `[sidebar, agent]`. The `forward-columns` entry is removed from `tokens.json`. No alias. `ProductNameTokenTests` fails when any of the 16 names, or the `forward-columns` entry, comes back.
- **New: the gallery shows one named scene per run (GL40 scaffold).** `swift run GlamorGallery --scene <name>`; with no flag it shows `components`, the same content as v0.3.1. `--scene=<name>` works too. A name it does not know, or an argument that starts with `--scene` and gives no name, stops with status 64 and one line on stderr. The gallery's window is 460 by 560 (it was 460 by 460).
- **Changed: the gallery's screenshots are `glamor-app-<scene>-<mode>.png`.** With `GLAMOR_GALLERY_SHOTS=<dir>` the file names carry the scene (they were `glamor-app-<mode>.png`).
- **New: the window check keeps one control list per scene (GL40 scaffold, GL43).** `scripts/window/WindowCheck.swift` takes the scene as its last argument and reads that scene's expected controls, Tab order and modes; `scripts/check-window.sh` has a `scene <name>` line before its `run` lines. The `components` list is the v0.3.1 list plus the ToggleCard switch (GL43): its name, value, press, Tab stop, switch-on track contrast and, because the small switch is under 24 by 24 pt, the distance to the nearest other control (a CHECK at 24 pt or more, WCAG 2.5.8). Run 3 read the switch as role AXCheckBox, subrole AXToggle, role description "toggle button", AXValue 1 and 32 by 18 pt; the list expects exactly that role and subrole, and `switch-ax` prints what was read on every run. Only the switch is the pointer target (`ToggleCard` draws no `contentShape` or tap on its row): WCAG 2.5.8 AA holds by the spacing line, and a `target-row` line measures the card row (title, help line and switch together) against 24 pt.
- **Breaking (look): buttons are capsules (GL41).** `PrimaryButtonStyle`, `SecondaryButtonStyle`, `GhostButtonStyle` (text and icon-only; an icon-only button is a circle), the glass main action (`glassMainAction`, Mac and iOS), `MenuPicker` and the `MoreMenu` hover are capsules: the corner is half the height. Heights do not change: 32, 28 and 24 (44 on iPhone and iPad). `Pill` was already a capsule; `CopyButton` and `ThemeSwitch`'s fallback follow by themselves. A `wide` ghost button (a menu row) keeps the row's rounded corner (r8, r6 when compact). Fields, `SearchBar`, rows, cards, banners and the segmented control stay rectangular with their radius, so a capsule button beside an r8 field is the intended mix. Chips keep the capsule they had.
- **New: `G.controlShape` (GL41).** The one capsule that every pressable style draws (`Capsule()`), so the shape cannot drift between styles. A button drawn by hand uses `G.controlShape` with `.focusRingCapsule()`. `PillButtonTests` fails when a pressable style holds a literal `Capsule()` again.
- **New: `focusRingCapsule()` and `focusRingCapsule(isFocused:)`.** The keyboard focus ring on a capsule, the second for a control that tracks its own focus (a `Menu`), as `focusRing(_:isFocused:)` does for a rounded rectangle.
- **Removed: `ButtonMetrics.radius`.** Internal; no button reads a radius from the metrics any more. No alias.
- **Fix: `MenuPicker`'s title is one line.** A long title truncates inside its 32 (or 28) high face instead of growing a second line.
- **New: the Pro and Soon marks (GL36).** `PlanMark` (`pro`, `soon`) and `Pill(mark:)`: an outlined capsule with the word (Pro, Soon; Indonesian Pro, Segera) and one VoiceOver element that reads "Pro, paid feature" or "Soon, not available yet" ("Pro, fitur berbayar", "Segera, belum tersedia"). Pro: a solid `accentEdge` outline, the word in `accentFg`. Soon: a dashed `border` outline, the word in `text2`. The word carries the meaning, not the colour and not the outline: the Soon outline is `border` at 1.10 to 1.40:1, so it is close to invisible and is decoration only. Both words hold 4.5:1 on the page, a panel, a card, a subtle well and a hovered card in light, dark and both Increase contrast modes (`ContrastTests`); the Pro outline holds 3:1. `Pill(text:color:)` is unchanged. Four catalog keys, en and id: `mark.pro`, `mark.proSpoken`, `mark.soon`, `mark.soonSpoken`. Place a mark inside a page next to the thing it names, never on a row of the list of places.
- **New: the `marks` gallery scene and its window-check list (GL36, GL40).** `swift run GlamorGallery --scene marks` shows each mark on the window and on a card (window 460 by 260). `scripts/check-window.sh` runs it in four modes and reads the words (4.5:1), the Pro outline (3:1), the Soon outline (reported, no claim) and the two spoken names. The `components` scene gets a `button-shape` line for Save, Cancel and Deploy (the corner pixel reads the ground, as a capsule leaves it), and the Deploy label read sits 8 pt in from its box (it was 5: a capsule's curve passes 0.44 pt inside that). The `components` control list does not change.
- **Fix: the window check looks controls up by what the tree holds, not by a guessed role.** The ToggleCard switch is found by its name in any role but static text, and its line says the role, subrole, frame and value it read; the Pro and Soon marks are found by what they read in any role (the line says which); the sidebar's folded-header line chooses Host before folding Targets (the press loop before it had ended on Settings, so "collapsed" alone was right) and adds the case of a folded header that does not hold the chosen place; a row asked for keyboard focus by a scene gets AXFocused, then Tab, like the generic loop, and a row that takes none prints the stops Tab visited; the Tab walk goes on past a stop it cannot name and says where it differed; `Cancel` is no longer read for the capsule corner (its fill reads like the window ground; `Save` and `Deploy` prove the shape, and all three draw `G.controlShape`); the `components` and `marks` strings runs print the whole accessibility tree (`TREE` lines); the marks window counts as up only once its texts are in the tree, and the marks scene prints its environment line. Nothing in `Sources/Glamor` changed.

- **New: `Sidebar`, the Mac sidebar frame, variant A (GL31).** The window's ground, a top row with the traffic lights' room and the Hide button, the caller's header (logo, name, `SidebarSearch`), a scrolling content area and a footer pinned under a hairline. `Sidebar(width:isResizable:onHide:header:content:footer:)`. The edge is the one `SplitDivider` (`G.Resize.sidebar`: 200 to 320, hides when dragged under the smallest); `isResizable: false` fixes the width, as the narrow window's column does. The content is a plain stack, not a lazy one: a lazy stack leaves rows below the fold out of the accessibility tree and the Tab order until they are scrolled to. VoiceOver reads one container, "Sidebar" ("Bilah samping"). The app saves the width (`SizeStore`), owns the Hide command (⌃⌘S) and supplies every word that is its own.
- **New: `SidebarItem` and `SidebarCount` (GL32).** A place: a 16 symbol, its name and, only where a person acts on it, a count; 32 high. Two inits: `SidebarItem(place:selection:count:)` for a `Place` of the registry that v0.3.1 added (GL35), and `SidebarItem(_:symbol:isSelected:count:help:action:)`. `SidebarCount(_:spoken:)` carries the number and the sentence VoiceOver reads ("2 transfers running"): Glamor does not know what is counted, the app writes the sentence. The chosen row is a `panel` fill, a `border` hairline and the `selectedMarker` capsule, not the `accentSubtle` fill of a list row. VoiceOver reads one name, the count's words as the value and the selected trait. There is no AXHelp on a row unless the app passes `help`: without one, the name is the tooltip of the clipped text alone, which is hidden from VoiceOver, so the name is not read twice.
- **New: `SidebarSection` (GL33).** A titled block of places that folds: a 32 high header in section-label type over its rows. Two ways to hold the open state: `isExpanded:` (the app's binding) or `id:` (kept per window in `SceneStorage`; a view in a bare `NSHostingView`, as the gallery's, has no scene and uses the binding). Two sidebars in one scene that share a section id pass a different `scope:` each. Folded rows are not drawn, so they leave the accessibility tree and the Tab order. A folded section that holds the chosen place takes `containsSelection: true` and `selectedTitle:`: its header shows a marker-coloured dot and reads "collapsed, <name> selected". The stored state's fields (`SidebarSectionStored`, `SidebarSectionBody`) are internal.
- **New: `SidebarSearch` (GL34).** A 32 high capsule at the top of the sidebar: a magnifier, the placeholder word and the shortcut badge (default ⌘K, repeated in the tooltip). It is a labelled button that calls `action`, which opens the app's own command palette. Typing and filtering are not built: it is not a text field and does not filter the sidebar. The title defaults to "Search" ("Cari"). VoiceOver reads one name; the badge is hidden.
- **Note: the sidebar reads the `PlaceRegistry` types of v0.3.1 (GL35).** Nothing new here: the registry itself shipped in v0.3.1.
- **New: `SidebarRail` and `SidebarColumn`, the narrow-window sidebar (GL38).** A rail 72 wide with one button per area (Home, each section of the `PlaceRegistry`, Settings) and the caller's footer pinned below, and a column of 184 for the chosen section's places. An area is 52 high, a glyph over its short title; the chosen one has the `panel` fill, the hairline and the marker, `G.Row.markerWidth` (4) wide, not 3. The rail and the column keep no state: the app owns the `area` binding and draws the column only for a section. Mac only; iPhone keeps the system tab bar.
- **New: `G.Width.rail` = 72 (GL38).** The one new token. The column is `G.Width.sidebar` less the rail. `tokens.json` has its `rail-width` entry.
- **New: `ProfileCard` and `ProfileCardStyle` (GL37).** The person at the bottom of the sidebar: a tile 32 circle on `avatar`, the name, the detail line and a chevron, in a popover of 260 for the app's menu. `.row` is the one bordered account card of the foot (`card` fill, `border` hairline, 12 radius at rest); `.avatar` is a 44 point circle for the rail. Both circles have a `border` hairline ring. VoiceOver reads one button, "name, detail"; the tooltip is on the hidden label, not on the button. Glamor knows no person: the name, the detail and the menu are the app's.
- **Changed: `SidebarItemStyle` takes a height, an alignment, a horizontal padding and a marker height and top** (internal; the rail draws the sidebar row look at 52 high, centred, with no side padding so a title has the button's 64, and the chosen marker beside the glyph).
- **New: `G.Row.railTitleScale` = 0.85 (GL38).** How far a rail item's title may shrink before it is cut: "Pengaturan" is 64.8 pt set at 12 and 55.1 at the floor.
- **New: the gallery scenes `sidebar-b` and `profile`, and their window-check lists (GL38, GL37).** The rail scene checks the rail's title and glyph, the chosen title and the marker in four modes, the names, 44 by 44 targets, AXPress and Space replacing the column, and the Tab order (rail, its footer, column). The profile scene checks the initial, the name and the detail in four modes, and that AXPress and Space open the menu popover, Escape closes it and focus returns to the card. `check-window.sh` has the run lines. They are unproven until the owner's run of `check-window.sh --strict`.
- **New: `SidebarItem` on iPad and iPhone (GL39); compiled and source-pinned only.** The same two inits, words and look as the Mac's, at least `G.touch` (44) high. The 44 is a floor: a larger text setting makes the row taller, and the symbol and the count scale with it. A press takes `G.Hover.pressed`; there is no hover, and the system draws keyboard focus. It is for the list inside an area on iPhone and the column of an iPad split view; `SidebarSection`, `SidebarSearch`, `Sidebar` and the rail are not built for iOS. iOS gains the tokens the row reads, named as on the Mac: `G.panel`, `G.field`, `G.placeholder`, `G.accentSubtle`, `G.selectedMarker`, `G.Hover.pressed`, `G.Space.s2`, `G.Row` (`padding`, `radius`, `markerWidth`, `markerInset`, `markerHeight`) and `G.Font.sectionLabel` with `sectionTracking`. `SidebarCount` moved to `Sources/Glamor/Places/` so both platforms read it. This row has never run on a device or in a simulator: CI builds it and its tests (GL56) but does not run them.
- **New: `G.Opacity.banner(_:)` (GL45), Mac and iOS.** The banner tint for the contrast in force: the constant at standard contrast and `G.Opacity.increased` with Increase contrast, as `G.Opacity.pill(_:)` does. `BannerOpacityTests` pin both. `Banner` and `FailureLine` still draw the constant `G.Opacity.banner`; they do not read the function yet.
- **Changed: CI builds the iOS tests (GL56).** `.github/workflows/checks.yml` runs `xcodebuild build-for-testing -scheme Glamor -destination 'generic/platform=iOS'` after the generic iOS build, so the `*+iOS` test files compile. They are not run.
- **New: the `sidebar-a` gallery scene and its window-check list (GL31 to GL34, GL40).** `swift run GlamorGallery --scene sidebar-a` draws the four parts together. `scripts/check-window.sh` has its run lines: names and roles, the selected row, the Tab order, Space, the folded section's dot and its value, no AXHelp on place rows, and that Tabbing to the last row keeps it inside the window. Unproven until the owner's run.

What an app does (Apps):

1. Bump Glamor to exactly 0.4.0 and rebuild.
2. Define the eight `G.Width` names in the app, in an `extension G` (they were komando's page widths).
3. Drop the `forward-columns` entry from your copy of the token notes, if you keep one.
4. Give the eight `G.Resize` areas their keys in the app (`ResizeArea(key:min:ideal:max:)`), and add them to the app's own list of areas where a test walks it.
5. Look at every button: toolbars and sheet rows where a button sits beside a field or a row, in light and dark and with Increase contrast. A button you draw by hand with `RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: 8)` or `6` becomes a Glamor style, or `G.controlShape` with `.focusRingCapsule()` (`.focusRingCapsule(isFocused:)` for a control that tracks its own focus). Remove your own corner overrides on buttons.
6. Use one word for "not built yet": `Pill(mark: .soon)` (Soon; Indonesian Segera). Rename the "Coming" rows and any tinted "Coming" pill to the mark. A paid feature takes `Pill(mark: .pro)`.
7. Build the sidebar from the parts (Mac): `Sidebar`, `SidebarSearch` (its `action` opens your command palette; Glamor does not filter), `SidebarSection` (pass `containsSelection:` and `selectedTitle:` when a folded section holds the chosen place; use a `scope:` when two sidebars share a section id) and `SidebarItem(place:selection:count:)` over your `PlaceRegistry`. Write the count's spoken sentence yourself. Do not pass a `help` unless it says more than the name.
8. For the narrow window use `SidebarRail` and, beside it for a section only, `SidebarColumn`; put `ProfileCard` (`.avatar` in the rail's footer, `.row` in the sidebar's) at the foot and supply the name, the detail line and the popover's menu. The app owns the `area` binding.
9. On iPad and iPhone use the same `SidebarItem` for the list inside an area. It has not run on a device: look at it with a larger text size and report what you see.
10. Run `scripts/check-window.sh --strict` (Keyboard navigation on) only after the owner's run of v0.4.0 has passed; the new scenes have no other proof.

## v0.3.1 - 2026-11-28

Semver level: PATCH (v0.3.1). No `G.*` name and no existing API changes; the one addition (GL35) is new public API in a patch, which breaks nothing because apps pin exact versions. Three colour and tint values change (the switch tint, the disabled main action, the ProgressBar track) and the window check reads more. Version files are not bumped here. The v0.3.1 tag waits for the owner's run of `scripts/check-window.sh --strict` (see GL30 below).

- **Added: `PlaceRegistry` types (GL35).** Groundwork for the sidebar in v0.4.0; no UI yet. Additive public API in a patch: apps pin exact versions, so nothing breaks.
- **Fix: `ToggleCard` tints its switch with `G.accentEdge` (GL27).** The system tint left the switch at 2.7:1 or less on a light card. `G.accentEdge` holds 4.2:1 or more on card, page and sunken in all four modes, held in `ContrastTests`. iOS has no Toggle in the library: an app tints its own with `G.accentEdge` (`guidelines/overview.md`).
- **Fix: the disabled main action reads in `text3` in every mode (GL28).** In dark it was 1.7 to 2.0:1 and is now 3.3 to 3.9:1. The glass main action fades its tint and rim, not the whole view.
- **Fix: `ProgressBar`'s track is `surfaceTrack`, not `G.border` (GL28).** No `ok`, `warn` or `danger` tone reaches 3:1 on `border` in Increase contrast. The fill is 3.6:1 or more on the track in four modes. The empty track in Increase contrast is 1.28:1 on the card, so the number carries the value. A stronger track returns with design-tokens `okEdge`, `warnEdge` and `dangerEdge` (rows DT19 and GL44).
- **Check: the window check asserts four more VoiceOver lines (GL30).** The theme switch name ("Switch to light theme") after the toggle; "Clear the search" after typing; the sheet title as a heading, through a new `sheet` window mode and the gallery flag `--open-sheet`; the unlabelled spinner "Working", read through its real role `AXBusyIndicator` (the check also accepts `AXProgressIndicator`). The first run on the owner's Mac (2026-11-28) showed two faults in the new lines, not in the library: the text was set by value instead of key events, and the spinner role was guessed. Both are fixed, and the second strict run passed: 98 PASS, 0 FAIL, 0 UNPROVEN (macOS 27.2.1, Keyboard navigation on). The check also reads what it finds when a line fails.
- **Rule: when a VoiceOver sitting is needed (GL30).** Only when a release changes a label, a role or a control. A release that changes only colours needs the window check and nothing more.

What an app does (Apps):

1. Bump Glamor to exactly 0.3.1 and rebuild.
2. A `Toggle` you draw yourself: tint it with `G.accentEdge` (`.tint(G.accentEdge)`).
3. Look at a disabled main action and a progress bar in dark and in Increase contrast.

## v0.3.0 - 2026-11-27

Semver level: MINOR (v0.3.0). The `G.*` names and the API stay; values change, six vendored constants disappear (nothing in Glamor read them), and `G.accentEdge` is new. While Glamor is used only inside Efolusi, a change of this kind ships as a minor, as design-tokens v0.8.0 does. Version files are not bumped here.

- **Breaking: the accent is the owl's copper, the `ember` ramp is gone (GL26).** Tokens are re-vendored from design-tokens v0.8.0 (`8e6c031`). `G.accent` is `#CD8F69` in both themes; `accentHover` `#DBA582`, `accentPressed` `#BD805A`, `accentSubtle` `#F4E9D2` (light), `onAccent` is ink `#1E1A14` in both themes (6.37:1 on copper), `accentFg`, `selectedMarker`, `focus` and `link` follow. The main action's label was white on the light theme; it is ink now.
- **New: `G.accentEdge`** (design-tokens `accent.edge`, Mac and iOS). Copper is 2.1 to 2.7:1 as a shape on a light ground, so a shape drawn with the accent uses it: 3:1 or more on page, card, sunken, track and `border` in light, dark and both Increase contrast modes (light `#A0693C`, dark the copper itself, Increase contrast `#443D32` and `#EFCFAC`). Glamor draws it as the 1px rim of the main button (`PrimaryButtonStyle`, `glassMainAction` on Mac and iOS), faded with the fill when disabled, and as the default fill of `ProgressBar`. In dark it is the fill's own colour: on the solid button no line shows; on the translucent glass main action the 1px line is drawn the same way and may show against the tint, which the window check looks at. iOS also gets `G.Line.hair`, as the Mac has.
- **Fix: `ProgressBar`'s default tone** was `G.accent` (2.24:1 on its `border` track in light); it is `G.accentEdge` (3.79:1). The `ok`, `warn` and `danger` tones are unchanged.
- **Tests and checks follow the new rule, none removed.** `ContrastTests` hold `accentEdge` at 3:1 on page, card, sunken, track and `border` in all four modes; the accent as text is `G.link` at 4.5:1 on every ground a link sits on (`statusColorsReadAsTextOnACard` reads `G.link` where it read `G.accent`); the label test runs in all four modes. `AccentEdgeSourceTests` pin where the rim is drawn and that no view reads `accentWarm` or `accentSoft` (2.89:1 and 1.83:1: text or decoration only). `scripts/check-colors.sh` also fails on `.fill(G.accent)`, `.strokeBorder(G.accentHover)`, `tone: Color = G.accent` and any use of `accentWarm` or `accentSoft` outside the token files.
- **Docs: `tokens.json`, `guidelines/overview.md` and the Button, Pill, ProgressBar, Chip and TextField notes** state the copper accent, the `accentEdge` rule, `link` for accent text (with an underline in running text) and the restated pill numbers (every status colour 4.5:1 or more on its pill; warning, 4.54:1 in light, is the lowest; the old 4.6 claim was out of date). New in `tokens.json`: `accentEdge` and `link`. The re-vendor also carries `text3xs`, `text4xs`, `controlHSmPlus` and `controlHMdPlus` (design-tokens DT01 and DT02); Glamor does not use them yet.

What an app does (Apps):

1. Bump Glamor to exactly 0.3.0 and rebuild.
2. Remove every use of `GlamorTokens.Color.ember50`, `ember100`, `ember150`, `ember300`, `ember600` and `ember700` (removed, no alias; Glamor itself never read them).
3. Check your own colours that named ember or drew the old accent: a shape drawn with `G.accent` on a light ground (a switch or checkbox you tint, a chart line, a tab underline) becomes `G.accentEdge`; the accent as text becomes `G.link`; a Pill or other tone that used `G.accent` for a word becomes `G.link` or `G.accentFg`.
4. Do not set your app's `AccentColor` to copper: a system `Toggle` then takes copper, which is 2.7:1 on a light card. Leave it, or tint the control with `G.accentEdge`.
5. Look at the main action, checked states and progress bars in light and dark, also with Increase contrast.
6. Run `scripts/check-window.sh --strict` with Keyboard navigation on.

## v0.2.0 - 2026-11-26

- **New: localisation, en and id (GL14).** Every word the library says itself (VoiceOver names, tooltips, the Copied announcement, spoken sizes, banner kinds, the defaults of `help`, `label` and `allTitle`) is in `Sources/Glamor/Resources/Localizable.xcstrings` with English and Indonesian, and follows the host app's languages first (`Bundle.main.preferredLocalizations`, so an English-only app on an Indonesian system stays English), then the person's language list, then English. `LocalisationTests` fails when a catalog string lacks `en` or `id`, when the two differ in placeholders, or when a source file says a user-facing word outside the catalog. The gallery has its own catalog. `Package.swift` declares `defaultLocalization: "en"` and the resources. The targets ship `en.lproj` and `id.lproj` `Localizable.strings` that `scripts/generate-strings.py` writes from the catalog (a test fails when they differ), because Swift 6.3.3's native build system copies a `.xcstrings` without compiling it.
- **New: `ThemeSwitchWords.current`**, the words in the language Glamor picks (the app's, then the person's). `ThemeSwitch` still needs its words (GL16); `english` and `indonesian` now read the catalog too.
- **Fix: a spinner built without a label has a name (GL22).** `SpinnerSlot`, `BusyLine` and `DelayedProgress` took `label: String = ""` and gave VoiceOver an unnamed spinner. `label` is now `String? = nil` and falls back to the catalog's "Working" ("Memproses"); pass your own label when the words beside the spinner already name it. `CopyButton` and `MoreMenu` `help` and `ChipRow` `allTitle` work the same way.
- **Fix: `ChipRow` drew an empty chip for "All".** Its initialiser's `allTitle` defaulted to an empty string; it now defaults to the catalog's "All" ("Semua").
- **Docs: glass turns grey when the window is not key (GL20).** The README now says this is the system's inactive glass state and expected: Deploy, the segments and the theme switch lose their tint until the window is key. No behaviour changed.

## v0.1.0 - 2026-11-23

- **New: warm glass (GL13).** System glass behind `#available(iOS 26, macOS 26, *)` on bars and sheets, `.glass` on the theme switch, the segmented control and the search field, and a tinted ember glass for the main action. Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast swap to the solid path, which is also what macOS 15 and iOS 18 draw. Materials come from design-tokens v0.7.0 (`scripts/vendor-tokens.sh`).
- **New: CI (GL15).** `.github/workflows/checks.yml` runs on every pull request and every push to `main`, on GitHub-hosted runners: the vendored tokens check, the colour, motion and size checks, `swift test`, and the generic iOS build.
- **New: the window check (`scripts/check-window.sh`).** Runs `GlamorGallery` on macOS 26 and checks the real window with no person at the keyboard: contrast in pixels, the label-to-icon gap, VoiceOver strings and `AXPress`, Tab order, Space and Return. It captures only that window and changes no system setting. `scripts/release-gate.sh` runs it after the CI checks; CI does not run it.
- **Fix: the search placeholder is readable on glass (GL18).** New `G.placeholder` (design-tokens `textMuted`): `SearchBar` and `FilterField` draw their placeholder in it instead of the system's colour. ContrastTests hold it at 4.5:1 or more on every surface a field sits on and on glass over them, light and dark (lowest 5.48:1).
- **Fix: the main action's label reads on its ember fill (GL19).** `glassMainAction` no longer uses the system's `.glassProminent`, whose own label colour was light on the dark theme's ember (2.05:1). It draws its own tinted glass and sets the label to `G.onAccent`: white on the light ember (5.24:1), the dark ink on the dark ember (8.43:1). The shape is the solid primary button's (32 high, `r8`), no longer a capsule.
