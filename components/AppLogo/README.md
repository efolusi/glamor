# AppLogo

Komando's mark, K-caret: a K whose stem is the cursor, on a graphite square. In Swift: `AppLogo(size: 28)` (to be redrawn from this spec).

**Provide:** the size: 28 beside the name in the sidebar, 20 where it is small, 48 on the Upgrade sheet and in alerts.

- The square's corner is 25% (continuous). The stem is `logo-cursor`, the arms `logo-ink` at stroke 10 (12 in the small drawing used at 32 and below), optically corrected so the arms and stem look equally heavy, on `logo-ground-top` to `logo-ground-bottom`.
- Same colors in both themes.
- For the Dock, Finder and anything outside the app, use the app icon under Logos, which adds Apple's grid, a top light, a hairline and a soft shadow.
- Don't recolor it, outline it, or set the K without its square.
